If your license key is not activating, the two most common causes are a full site quota and an inactive subscription. Work through the checks below in order before contacting support.

## Check your site quota

Every license allows activation on a limited number of sites. If the quota is full, new activations are rejected even with a valid key.

1. Log in to your account at [wpaxiom.com/account](https://wpaxiom.com/account)
2. Open the **Licenses** tab
3. Find your license key and expand it — you will see a list of all currently activated sites
4. If the list is full, click **Deactivate** next to any site you no longer use
5. Return to your WordPress dashboard and try activating again

<Callout type="tip">
  Deactivating a site from the Licenses tab frees up a slot immediately — no waiting period. Use this whenever you migrate a site, move to a new domain, or retire a staging environment.
</Callout>

## Check your subscription status

A lapsed or cancelled subscription will prevent activation and may deactivate existing sites.

1. In your account, open the **Subscriptions** tab
2. Locate your Axiom Blocks subscription and check the **Status** and **Next payment** date
3. If the subscription is **expired** or **on-hold**, renew it from the same page
4. Once renewed, return to WordPress and activate your license key again

## Check the license key itself

- Make sure you copied the full key with no leading or trailing spaces — paste it directly from your account page rather than typing it manually
- Each license key is tied to the product you purchased. If you have multiple licenses, confirm you are using the key for Axiom Blocks specifically

## Firewall or outbound request blocked

License activation makes an outbound HTTP request from your server to our licensing service. Some managed hosts block outgoing requests by default.

- Ask your host to confirm that outbound HTTP connections are permitted from your server
- If you are on a local development environment with no public URL, activation may fail because the site cannot be verified — use a staging or live domain instead
