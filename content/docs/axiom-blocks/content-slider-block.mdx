Content Slider turns **any blocks** into a slider or carousel — each slide is a container you can fill with images, text, buttons, or whole layouts. It runs on a lightweight, no-jQuery engine (no bundled library), with slide, fade, and coverflow effects, autoplay, arrows, pagination, drag/swipe, and an optional click-to-zoom lightbox.

## How to add

Open the block inserter, switch to the **Axiom Blocks** category, and click **Content Slider** (hover it for a live preview). It drops in with a couple of starter slides. Select a slide and add whatever blocks you want inside it; use the block toolbar to add or remove slides.

## Effects

Pick a transition in the **Slider** panel:

- **Slide** — the classic horizontal (or vertical) track.
- **Fade** — slides cross-fade in place.
- **Coverflow** — a 3D, depth-stacked carousel.

Set **Orientation** to **Horizontal** or **Vertical** for the slide and coverflow effects.

## Slides per view & scrolling

- **Slides per view** — show one slide at a time or several side by side, set independently for **desktop**, **tablet**, and **mobile**.
- **Slides to scroll** — how many slides advance per step.
- **Gap** — the space between slides.
- **Adaptive height** — the slider grows and shrinks to fit the current slide instead of using a fixed height.

## Autoplay

Turn on **Autoplay** and set the **delay** between slides. Options include **Loop** (wrap around to the start), **Pause on hover**, and an optional **play/pause button** for accessibility. **Transition speed** controls how fast each slide animates.

## Navigation

- **Arrows** — previous/next controls with an adjustable **offset** and their own **colour** and **background**.
- **Pagination** — choose **dots**, a **fraction** (`2 / 6`), or a **progress bar**, with colour controls for the resting and active states.
- **Drag & swipe** — pointer and touch dragging, on by default.
- **Keyboard** — arrow keys move between slides when the slider is focused.

## Lightbox

Enable **Lightbox** to make images inside slides open in a full-screen zoom view, with keyboard (arrow keys and <kbd>Esc</kbd>) navigation.

## Styling

Give the slider a fixed **height**, a **border** (colour, width, radius), and per-side **padding** and **margin**. Content Slider supports **Wide** and **Full** alignment plus an HTML anchor. Motion is disabled automatically when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

<Callout type="tip">
  Building a testimonial or logo carousel? Set **Slides per view** higher on desktop and lower on mobile so it stays readable on small screens.
</Callout>

## HTML output

```html
<div class="ab-slider ab-slider--slide" data-autoplay="1" data-loop="1">
  <div class="ab-slider__viewport">
    <div class="ab-slider__track">
      <!-- each slide renders your inner blocks -->
    </div>
  </div>
  <!-- arrows + pagination render here -->
</div>
```

The `ab-slider--slide|fade|coverflow` and `ab-slider--vertical` modifier classes drive the effect and orientation; behaviour is read from `data-*` attributes by the view script, and colours, gap, height, and border are `--ab-slider-*` custom properties on the wrapper. Each slide keeps its own inner-block markup.
