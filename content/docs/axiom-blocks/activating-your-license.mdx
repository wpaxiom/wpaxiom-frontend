Once the Axiom Blocks Pro plugin is installed, you need to activate your license key to unlock Pro features. Activation takes a few seconds.

## Find your license key

Your key is available in two places:

- **Your account** — log in at [wpaxiom.com/account](/account), open the **Licenses** tab, and copy the key from the Axiom Blocks Pro row
- **Purchase email** — the license key is included in the order confirmation email sent after purchase

Keys follow the format `AXM-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`. Copy the full key exactly as shown — it is case-sensitive.

## Activate in WP admin

1. In WP admin, go to **Axiom Blocks** in the sidebar
2. Open the **Settings** tab
3. Find the **Pro License** card
4. Paste your key into the input field
5. Click **Activate**

When activation succeeds the notice below the input updates to **License active — valid until {date}.** If your subscription has no fixed end date the notice reads **valid until further notice**. The input becomes read-only and the button switches to **Deactivate**.

<Callout type="info">
  The Pro License card only appears when the Axiom Blocks Pro plugin is installed and active. If you don't see it, check that the Pro plugin is activated under **Plugins**.
</Callout>

## Activation errors

| Message | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| Invalid license key — please check the key and try again. | Re-copy the key from your account page; paste directly rather than typing to avoid extra spaces |
| Could not reach the license server. Try again in a moment. | Your server cannot reach the licensing service — check that outbound HTTP is permitted and retry |
| Limit reached | Site quota is full — log in to your account, open the **Licenses** tab, and remove a site to free a slot |

## Automatic license checks

The plugin re-validates your license in the background every time an admin page loads (at most once per hour). If you remove a site from your account dashboard, the WordPress site will automatically detect the change within an hour and the license card will return to the inactive state — you do not need to click Deactivate first.

## Deactivate a site

To move your license to a different domain, deactivate it on the current site first.

**From WP admin:**

1. Go to **Axiom Blocks → Settings**
2. Click **Deactivate** in the Pro License card
3. The slot is freed immediately — activate on the new site

**From your account:**

1. Log in at [wpaxiom.com/account](/account) and open the **Licenses** tab
2. Find the site under your license and click the remove icon
3. The slot is freed without needing access to the old site
