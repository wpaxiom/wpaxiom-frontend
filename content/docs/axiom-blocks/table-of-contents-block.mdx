Table of Contents builds an automatic, linked index from the **headings already on your page** — no manual list to maintain. It scans the content, adds anchor links, and keeps the list in sync as you edit. It adds smooth scrolling, active-section highlighting, a sticky sidebar, a collapsible panel, and a mobile bar on top.

## How to add

Open the block inserter, switch to the **Axiom Blocks** category, and click **Table of Contents**. Place it wherever the index should appear — inline near the top of an article, or in a sidebar column. The editor previews the list live from your page's headings; on the front end the links and anchors are generated automatically.

## Choosing headings

In the **Structure** panel, toggle which heading levels to include (**H1**–**H6**; H2–H4 by default). Only the levels you enable appear, and deeper levels are indented to show the hierarchy. Each included heading gets a stable `id` so its anchor link keeps working — existing anchors are preserved.

## Markers

- **Numbered** — sequential numbers in a mono typeface, with an optional **number prefix** (for example, `Step `).
- **Bullet** — a small dot.
- **None** — text only, aligned to the rail.

## Header

Set an **eyebrow** label (defaults to *On this page*) and, optionally, a larger **title** below it with its own heading level. When the panel is collapsible you can show a **section count** next to the eyebrow.

## Behaviour

The **Behaviour** panel controls the interactive layer (all progressive enhancement — it degrades to plain anchor links):

- **Smooth scroll** with an adjustable **scroll offset** to clear a fixed header.
- **Highlight active section** — the current heading is marked as you scroll (scroll-spy).
- **Reading-progress rail** — a sliding accent rail follows the section in view.
- **Collapsible** — a chevron folds the list to its header; optionally **start collapsed**.
- **Sticky on scroll** — pin the panel with a **top offset**; a long list scrolls **inside** the panel instead of running off-screen. Optionally **disable sticky on mobile**, or keep the **mobile dock bar** that opens the list as a bottom sheet and folds away after you jump.
- **Copy-link** on each heading, **footer back-to-top**, and a **floating back-to-top** button.

## Colours

Choose a **Light** or **Dark** scheme as the starting point, then override any part — background, link, hover, active section, marker, progress, and border — with your own colours. Border width, corner radius, nesting indent, and item spacing are adjustable too.

<Callout type="tip">
  For a docs-style layout, place the Table of Contents in a narrow sidebar column, turn on **Sticky** and **Highlight active section**, and it will track the reader down the page.
</Callout>

## HTML output

```html
<nav class="ab-toc ab-toc--numbered ab-toc--sticky" aria-label="Table of contents">
  <div class="ab-toc__head">…eyebrow + optional title…</div>
  <div class="ab-toc__body">
    <ul class="ab-toc__list-ul"><!-- generated links --></ul>
  </div>
</nav>
```

The `ab-toc--numbered|bullet|none`, `ab-toc--sticky`, `ab-toc--progress`, and `ab-toc--dark` modifier classes drive markers, placement, the progress rail, and the colour scheme; colours and spacing are `--ab-toc-*` custom properties on the wrapper. Heading `id`s are injected into your content so the anchors resolve.
